## Hypotheses and fixed thresholds
- H1 (calibration): On micro-SME document sets with known true values, 90% intervals from the discrete-mixture model have empirical coverage in [0.85, 0.95]. The pedigree/lognormal baseline (I-02 method) is reported next to it. FAIL if mixture coverage < 0.85.

- H3 (ablation): Removing the discrete layer worsens coverage by >= 5 percentage points on sets with >= 1 ambiguous document. FAIL if < 5 points.
