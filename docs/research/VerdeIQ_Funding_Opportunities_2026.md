### 2.1 EIC Accelerator (European Innovation Council)
- **Funder:** European Commission / EISMEA, under Horizon Europe
- **Type:** Hybrid — blended grant (up to €2.5M, non-dilutive) + equity investment (up to ~€10M) via EIC Fund
- **Ticket size:** Grant below €2.5M; equity component negotiated, dilution varies (typically 10-25% combined with grant scaling)
- **Status:** Open call, rolling short-proposal submissions; EIC Work Programme 2026 published, total accelerator budget ~€1.2B+ across 2026, plus €220M for "Challenges" topics (incl. Deep Tech for Climate Adaptation). Cut-off dates roll through 2026 — **verify next cut-off** at source.
- **Source:** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en , https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator/eic-accelerator-challenges-2026_en
- **Eligibility:** Single SME/startup, EU-based (Cyprus qualifies), TRL 6-8, innovation with market-creating potential. VerdeIQ's AI-driven CSRD/CBAM automation is a strong "deep tech meets regulation" story.
- **Fit score:** 8/10 — MVP stage may be borderline for TRL 6-8 threshold; better suited once VerdeIQ has paying customers and defensible AI IP. Strongly consider the "Deep Tech for Climate Adaptation" challenge and general "Open" pillar.
- **Effort:** Very high. Short proposal (5 pages) first, then full proposal + interview if invited. 2-4 months minimum; often 6+ with resubmissions.
- **Success rate:** Historically ~5-8% for short proposals passing to full proposal stage; ~65% success at interview stage for those invited (2024/2025 EIC data, verify current).
- **Strategic value:** EU-wide credibility stamp ("EIC-backed"), access to EIC Business Acceleration Services (coaching, investor matchmaking, Seal of Excellence networking).
- **Pitch angle:** Position VerdeIQ as critical infrastructure for CSRD/CBAM compliance across 15M+ EU SMEs facing new reporting mandates — a market-creating, not just efficiency, innovation.

## 7. Red Flags / Traps to Avoid

- **Do not assume MVP-stage is "TRL 6-8."** EIC Accelerator explicitly requires TRL 6-8 (near-market readiness). An early-stage MVP with limited paying customers may be rejected on TRL grounds — build revenue/customer evidence before applying, or apply to Women TechEU / RIF first to season the "market validation" story.
- **Consortium-based calls (Horizon Cluster 5, Digital Europe, LIFE, Interreg) require significant lead time.** Do not attempt to build a multi-country consortium from scratch inside a 6-8 week deadline window — start partner search via EEN months in advance.
- **EU Innovation Fund is a poor direct fit.** It is designed for capital-intensive industrial decarbonization deployment, not SaaS. Don't waste cycles applying as lead applicant; instead pursue Innovation Fund awardees as sales prospects.
- **Watch out for "vouchers exhausted" traps** — as with EUIPO SME Fund Vouchers 1 & 2 already exhausted for 2026. Apply to any voucher-based, first-come-first-served scheme immediately upon opening, not after building a full grant strategy around it.
- **Geographic eligibility traps** — Techstars Alabama, Sustainable Ventures UK, and some Rockstart/Antler residency programmes require physical relocation or are non-EU/geographically restricted. Confirm residency/geography rules before investing application effort.
- **Equity dilution stacking** — Rockstart, Antler, and the equity component of EIC Accelerator all take equity. Model cumulative dilution carefully before pursuing multiple equity-based programmes in parallel; prioritize equity-free grants (RIF, Women TechEU, LIFE, Digital Europe, Horizon) first.
- **RRP/national funding sunset risk** — Cyprus's Recovery and Resilience Plan runs through 2026; RRP-funded schemes (e.g., Digital Upgrade of Enterprises) may not be renewed post-2026. Act on any open RRP-linked window promptly; don't wait for a "next cycle" that may not exist under RRF rules.
- **Draft work programmes are not final** — some Horizon Europe Cluster 5 2026-2027 documents (e.g., the FFG-hosted PDF) are explicitly marked as drafts "not adopted or endorsed by the European Commission." Always cross-check against the official Funding & Tenders Portal before treating any topic/deadline as final.
- **Do not over-index on brand-name accelerators with poor thematic fit.** Elemental Excelerator, Katapult (Africa-focused), and Techstars WaterTech (US/Alabama-focused) all have compelling brand value but weak practical fit for a Cyprus-based SaaS company; time is better spent on EU-native, higher-fit programmes.
- **Verify every deadline directly before submission.** Search snapshots and third-party aggregators (e.g., euro-access.eu, fundwiser.eu) can lag behind the authoritative Funding & Tenders Portal, EIC site, or RIF/IRIS portal. Treat all dates in this report as **indicative** until reconfirmed at the primary source URL listed.
