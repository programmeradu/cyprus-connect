## 1. What VerdeIQ Is

VerdeIQ is a Cyprus-native sustainability platform that helps small and medium enterprises measure, reduce, and report their environmental impact — in EUR, in Greek and English, and against the specific regulatory calendar that applies to Cyprus (CSRD, ESRS, VSME, CBAM, EU Taxonomy, Cyprus VAT, EAC grid factors).

It is not a re-skinned global carbon tool. Every calculation, tariff, deadline, and default reflects the Cyprus context — an isolated island grid with high carbon intensity (~610 gCO₂/kWh), regulated by CERA, supplied largely by EAC, and reporting into EU directives with the compressed timelines that apply to Wave 2 and Wave 3 entities.

## 2. Why Cyprus, Why Now

- **Regulatory pressure is landing.** ~40 Cypriot entities fall inside CSRD Wave 2 (FY2025 reporting), and thousands more SMEs are pulled in indirectly as suppliers to EU customers who must report Scope 3.
- **The tools don't fit.** Global ESG platforms ship with UK/DE/FR defaults, EU-average grid factors, English-only output, and no awareness of Registrar of Companies filing patterns. On Cyprus, that produces materially wrong Scope 2 numbers and unusable domestic reports.
- **The SME segment is underserved.** Enterprise ESG suites price out companies below ~250 employees. Cyprus is dominated by SMEs — shipping, hospitality, financial services, professional services, light manufacturing — that need something priced and scoped for them.
- **Bilingual is a filter, not a feature.** Domestic disclosures, board packs, and public communication default to Greek. Investor and counterparty requests default to English. VerdeIQ ships both.

## 4. Product Pillars

### 4.1 Measure
- Scope 1, 2, 3 emissions with Cyprus-correct emission factors
- Document upload + OCR for utility bills, fuel invoices, travel receipts
- Accounting integrations (QuickBooks, Xero) for spend-based estimation
- Real-time grid intensity for location-based Scope 2

### 4.2 Report
- CSRD / ESRS mapped disclosures (E1-E5, S1-S4, G1)
- VSME (voluntary SME standard) template — the practical entry point for smaller Cypriot companies
- CBAM quarterly report generator
- EU Taxonomy alignment checker
- Double materiality assessment tool
- Every report exportable in Greek and English

### 4.3 Reduce
- Prioritised action list ranked by €/tCO₂e for Cyprus conditions
- Energy cost calculator using EAC tariffs, not EU averages
- Peer benchmarking against sector medians
- Science-Based Targets pathway modelling

### 4.4 Learn
- Bilingual pillar guides written in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English
- Glossary of ~50+ routable terms (CSRD, ESRS, CBAM, Scope 1/2/3, Net Zero, etc.)
- Awareness content for teams new to the topic
- Context-aware assets on every page — no stock imagery

### 4.5 Comply
- Compliance checker keyed to Cyprus entity types
- Regulatory deadline tracker (next EU deadline surfaced on the landing page)
- Audit trail on every data point

## 5. What Makes It Different

1. **Cyprus-correct by default.** EAC grid factor, CERA regulated tariffs, EUR + 19% VAT, Asia/Nicosia timezone, Registrar of Companies awareness. No configuration required.
2. **Bilingual as a first-class citizen.** Every route, every report, every email in EN and EL with proper hreflang. Not machine-translated afterthoughts.
3. **SME-priced.** Transparent EUR pricing, no "contact sales" gates for the plans SMEs actually buy.
4. **Editorial, not vibecoded.** Sharp typography, hand-drawn accent illustrations, real photography, no gradient soup. Reflects the seriousness of an audit deliverable.
5. **AI where it earns its keep.** Gemini-powered course generation, document parsing, insight suggestions — never as a chatbot gimmick.
6. **Own the data.** EU data residency, GDPR-first, export everything at any time.

- **Q2:** Direct EAC billing ingestion (API or scraper) so Scope 2 is zero-input. Cyprus Statistical Service integration for sector benchmarks.

## 8. Vision — 3 Year Horizon

- **The default choice for Cyprus SMEs facing CSRD, VSME, or CBAM.** When a Cypriot accountant is asked "which tool", the answer is VerdeIQ.
- **The public reference on Cyprus decarbonisation.** Free public dashboards for grid mix, sector emissions, and regulatory calendar drive top-of-funnel and cement authority.
- **A network effect through supply chains.** Every VerdeIQ customer's Scope 3 questionnaire becomes an on-ramp for their Cypriot suppliers.
- **Adjacent regulatory surfaces.** Pillar 3 ESG for banks, SFDR for asset managers, EED for energy-intensive users — same data, additional output formats.
- **Marketplace.** Vetted Cyprus-based providers for verified reductions: solar installers, energy auditors, EV fleet, waste operators, verified offsets.

## 9. Non-Goals

- **Not global.** No Africa, no US, no APAC coverage. The scope is Cyprus and the EU frameworks a Cyprus entity must file into.
- **Not enterprise-only.** No 6-month implementations, no per-seat pricing above what an SME will pay.
- **Not a consultancy.** Software with embedded expertise, not a services front-end.
- **Not a chatbot.** AI assists specific workflows; it does not replace the report.

## 10. Design & Content Principles

- **Anti-vibecode.** No gradient soup, no glassmorphism as decoration, no radial glows, no placeholder letters as logos. Solid foregrounds, editorial serifs, real photography, hand-drawn accent line-art.
- **ASD-STE100.** All long-form content (articles, pillars, glossary, learn, docs) follows Simplified Technical English: approved vocabulary, active voice, ≤20-word procedure sentences, one meaning per word, no idioms, no em-dashes in body copy.
- **Context-aware assets.** Every marketing / learn / pillar page ships with generated hero + inline visuals specific to the subject. No stock clichés.
- **Never lazy.** Verify full content fits. No truncation, no overflow, mobile checked in the first pass.
- **Bilingual parity.** Every page ships EN + EL with correct hreflang.
