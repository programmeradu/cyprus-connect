## What VerdeIQ Actually Does — With Real Examples

Think of VerdeIQ as three things stacked on top of each other. You can use just one. You can use all three.

### 1. It Measures — the way your accountant measures profit

You already know how much you spent on electricity last year, because it is in your books. VerdeIQ takes that same information and does the maths that turns "€14,200 in electricity" into "27.8 tonnes of CO₂" — using the correct Cyprus grid number, not a European average.

**What that feels like:**

Maria runs a 22-room boutique hotel in Paphos. Every month she uploads her EAC bill, her diesel receipt for the generator, and her laundry service invoice. VerdeIQ reads the PDFs, pulls out the numbers, and updates her footprint automatically. It takes her about 8 minutes.

When her German tour operator sent the annual sustainability questionnaire this year, Maria opened VerdeIQ, clicked "Export supplier report," and sent back a one-page PDF in 40 seconds. Last year the same questionnaire took her three weekends and a €900 consultant.

**The wow moment:** she saw that her diesel generator — running maybe 15 hours a month during summer outages — was responsible for 11% of her footprint. She switched to a battery backup, and the number dropped the next month. She could see it drop.

### 2. It Reports — the boring paperwork, done

The EU has invented several new report formats with names that sound like passwords: CSRD, VSME, CBAM, ESRS. You do not need to memorise any of them. VerdeIQ knows which one you need based on your size and what you do, and it fills the form for you.

**What that feels like:**

Andreas owns a family cement business near Larnaca. He exports about 40% of what he makes to Italy. Last October his Italian customer forwarded him an email: "Starting January, we need your CBAM quarterly reports or we cannot buy from you."

Andreas had never heard of CBAM. He called his accountant, who had also never heard of it. He was quoted €6,000 by a consultancy to "help him set up." Instead he tried VerdeIQ.

He entered his customs codes (which were already on his invoices), his production tonnage (already in his ERP), and his fuel use (already on the tax return). VerdeIQ produced a CBAM report in the exact format the EU wants, with the correct emission values, in Greek for his records and English for the customer.

Total time on VerdeIQ: about 90 minutes over two evenings. Total cost that quarter: less than the consultant charged for the first phone call.

**The wow moment:** the platform showed him that if he switched 20% of his fuel to biomass, he would drop below the CBAM charge threshold entirely by 2027. That is a strategic decision he can now actually make.

### 3. It Reduces — where the money is

Measuring is only useful if it leads to something. VerdeIQ ranks the things you could do to cut emissions, but ranks them the way a business owner thinks: **euros saved per tonne of CO₂ avoided, with payback in months, using Cyprus prices.**

Not "install solar" as a slogan. Instead: "Install 42 kW of rooftop solar. Cost after the grant you qualify for: €18,400. Payback: 4.2 years. Saves 34 tonnes a year. Here are three certified installers in your district."

**What that feels like:**

Elena runs a logistics firm in Limassol — 14 vans, 3 trucks. VerdeIQ looked at her fuel receipts and suggested three things, in order of return:

1. Fit tyre-pressure monitors on the fleet (€2,100, saves €4,800 a year in fuel)
2. Route optimisation software (€180/month, saves €1,600/month)
3. Replace the two oldest vans with electric when the current lease ends in 14 months (the grant covers 40%)

She did the first two the following week. The third is on her calendar. She did not need a consultant to tell her what a spreadsheet already showed — she needed the spreadsheet to be built for her, and to know which grants she qualifies for.

**The wow moment:** VerdeIQ told her about a Cyprus Ministry of Transport grant for electric commercial vehicles that her accountant did not know existed. That grant alone paid for her VerdeIQ subscription 30 times over.
