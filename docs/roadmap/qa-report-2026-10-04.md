# **Executive summary**

## **Not ready for pilot customers yet; demo-able to investors on a guided path**

The core idea works on screen: an EAC bill PDF is read correctly (kWh, dates, two-month split), a water bill and a bank CSV are read and categorised, CBAM import lines turn into a certificate estimate and an XML draft, the deadline engine applies 2026 EU law with sources, and most of the app has a Greek interface. That is a credible investor demo if you drive it yourself.

It is not ready for a paying pilot because four things a customer relies on are wrong or missing: the emission factors give different CO2 numbers for the same kWh in three places (0.535 in the app, 0.622 in the free tool, 0.489 in your own positioning); outbound email is not configured in production, so supplier questionnaires, reminders and invites can't be sent; Verde, the AI assistant, says no electricity data exists when it does and leaks internal tool names into answers; and there is no Sign out, Change password or Team section. A customer submitting a VSME or CBAM figure from today's build could submit the wrong number.

**Coverage:** 61 checklist components: 30 Pass with issues, 11 Fail, 8 Partly tested (rest Blocked), 7 Pass, 5 Blocked.

**Issues logged:** 124 in total: 4 Critical, 27 High, 55 Medium, 38 Low.

## **The 10 fixes that matter most, in order**

1. **Configure outbound email (F81).** Supplier data requests fail with 'Email sending is not set up yet (RESEND\_API\_KEY and EMAIL\_FROM)'. Reminders and invites will fail the same way, and the error leaks environment-variable names to customers.  
2. **Use one emission-factor table everywhere (F14, F115, F24, F104, F6).** Electricity is calculated at \~0.535 kg/kWh in the app, 0.622 in the free calculator, and 0.489 in Vuneli's messaging; water at 0.616, 0.539 and 0.344 kg/m³. The 1,840 kWh test bill comes out at 0.984 t in the app versus 0.900 t at 0.489 (+9.4%).  
3. **Fix Verde's data access and answer formatting (F52, F53).** Verde says there is no July/August kWh reading although the bill is saved, and prints '(read\_footprint, co2e\_total, Aug)' instead of a number.  
4. **Stop evidence tasks closing without evidence (F88, F57, F89).** 'Upload evidence for Scope 1' closes with one click and no file, which undermines the audit-trail promise.  
5. **Add Sign out, Change password and Team/Members (F74).** None exist. This also blocked the password-reset, Google sign-in, invite and role tests.  
6. **Make the fingerprint checkable end to end (F114, F80).** The app shows only 12 characters of each fingerprint, /verify needs all 64, and Vuneli's own CBAM draft fingerprint is 'Not in our register'.  
7. **Remove other companies from the leaderboard or make it opt-in (F100, F66).** A new workspace sees another customer ('StaUniverse') by name with its score.  
8. **Make bill intake robust (F20/F26, F21, F28/F29).** Phone photos of EAC bills fail every time; Greek diesel invoices are rejected so Scope 1 fuel can't be recorded; blank and corrupted PDFs get the wrong message.  
9. **Stabilise the dashboard and fix its claims (F96, F95, F51, F32, F45, F99).** Overview and reports APIs return 500/503 repeatedly; the bell shows 'nothing waiting' during an outage; Home says '+5837.5%' and 'No figures backed by records yet' after bills were read.  
10. **Align VAT with the 19% Cyprus promise (F68, F119).** Stripe checkout charged 21% Romanian VAT in RON from a non-Cyprus IP, while the app and Terms promise 19% Cyprus VAT.

# **Security and privacy findings**

* **Positive: account isolation holds at the API.** Requests for another user id (/api/users?id=…, /preferences, /api/analytics?userId=…) all return 403 'You can only access your own account.' Signed-out visits to /app routes redirect to sign-in.  
* **Leaderboard exposes other customers (High, F100/F66).** Workspace names and scores are shown to all users with no opt-in.  
* **Environment variable names exposed (Critical, F81).** An error message shows RESEND\_API\_KEY and EMAIL\_FROM.  
* **No sign-out or password change (High, F74).** Users on shared office computers can't end a session.  
* **Normal sessions call an admin endpoint (Low, F70).** GET /api/admin/billing/setup-prices returns 403 on every Billing load; harmless but shows admin routes to the client.  
* **Ids in URLs.** The user id appears in API query strings (/api/users?id=user\_…), not in page URLs. Acceptable given the 403 checks, but avoid logging these URLs.  
* **Vendor names in public endpoints.** /api/gemini/analyze and the Security page's hosting-platform names are public; a deliberate choice is advised (F122, F119).  
* **Legal identity.** The privacy policy names the controller 'Verde IQ' with no registration number or address (F119).  
* **Bank feed labelling.** Bank of Cyprus 'Ready to link' opens the bank's sandbox, not live, without a test-mode label (F61).  
* **Consent.** Analytics consent defaults to denied until the visitor chooses (positive); the banner offers only two buttons (F124).
