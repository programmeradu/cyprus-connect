---
name: No icons, no pills
description: Global UI ban on decorative Lucide-style icons and rounded pill/badge chips across all pages
type: constraint
---
Do not use decorative icons (Lucide, emoji, etc.) as UI accents — they read as generic/vibe-coded. Use typography, spacing, dividers, numerals, and color for hierarchy instead. **Why:** icons flatten a premium brand and mark work as AI-boilerplate.

Do not use rounded "pill" badges/chips (`rounded-full` label chips with a word inside) for tags, categories, filters, or status. Use underlined labels, small caps eyebrows, numeric prefixes, or bordered rectangular tabs. **Why:** pill chips are the #1 tell of vibe-coded UIs.

Exceptions (allowed): functional icons that carry meaning users need (search magnifier inside an input, close X on a modal, external-link arrow, expand chevron on an accordion). Even these should be minimal weight, single-stroke, monochrome.
